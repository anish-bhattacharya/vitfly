| **B+ Distill** | **1 crash** 🏆 | **3 crash** | Surpasses |

| Fusion Distill | 2 crash | 1 crash | Matches |

### Trees Environment (easier, lower discriminative power)

| Model | 5m/s | 7m/s | vs Teacher |
|-------|------|------|------------|
| Teacher ViT+LSTM | 0 crash | 0 crash | — |
| **B+ Distill** | **0 crash** 🏆 | **1 crash** | Matches or slightly worse |
| E Distill | 1 crash | 2 crash ✅ best | Worse at 7m/s |
| Fusion Distill | 0 crash | 1 crash | Matches or slightly worse |
| Most BC models | 0 crash | — | All match teacher |

### Critical Finding
**Distillation helps in hard environments, can hurt in easy environments at high speed.** Specifically:
- Spheres @ 5m/s: distilled B+/E (1 crash) < teacher (2 crash) ✅
- Spheres @ 7m/s: E (1 crash) < teacher (5 crash) ✅ Dramatic improvement
- Trees @ 7m/s: teacher (0 crash) < E (2 crash), B+ (1 crash), Fusion (1 crash) ❌ Degradation

## 3. Architecture Insights

### What We Got Wrong
1. **"E is best because it's pure SSM" ❌** — E's encoder is CNN, not SSM. Its advantage comes from temporal SSM processing, not from visual SSM.
2. **"SSM must be at both ends" ❌** — Pure SSM encoders (CoarseSSM) fail with simple MSE alignment. CNN encoders work because they preserve spatial structure.
3. **"MambaVision is hybrid SSM" ❌** — MambaVision's "SSM path" is just Linear→GELU→Linear (mini MLP). The real work is done by depthwise CNN.
4. **"Distillation never degrades" ❌** — True for spheres but false for trees @ 7m/s.

### What We Confirmed
1. **Cross-architecture distillation works** — with CNN-based encoders, MSE feature alignment succeeds
2. **Spatial structure matters** — TransMamba/CAB can bridge the gap for SSM encoders with direction-aware techniques
3. **B+ Aug BC (1 crash) = B+ Distill (1 crash)** — data augmentation can substitute for distillation on some architectures
4. **E is the most speed-robust and environment-robust model** — 1 crash in both environments at 5m/s, 1 crash in spheres at 7m/s
